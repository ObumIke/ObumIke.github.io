From Network Availability to Operational Excellence

Network availability is measured in percentages.
Operational excellence is measured in the experience behind those numbers.

Across telecom network operations, I have learned that keeping a network available is never just
about responding to alarms and restoring faults.

It requires an end-to-end view — RAN, transmission, fibre, core, power, and supporting
infrastructure — working together with disciplined processes and capable people.

My operational rhythm is simple and consistent:
Detect → Assess → Prioritize → Restore → Validate → Analyze → Prevent → Improve.

Restoration is important.
Excellence goes further.

It means understanding root causes, strengthening resilience, managing risk, learning from every
incident, and continuously improving both the systems and the teams responsible for service
delivery.

Behind every KPI is a customer who expects the network to work.

Operational excellence, therefore, is not simply about keeping infrastructure running.
It is about building a reliable, resilient, and continuously improving network that earns customer
confidence every single day.

That is the standard I hold myself to.

Be the light that serves, transforms, and ignites greatness for generations.

— Engr. Obumneme A. Ikechebelu
Telecom Network Operations & Transformation Leader
